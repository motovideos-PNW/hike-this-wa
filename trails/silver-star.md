\# Silver Star Mountain



\- \*\*Distance:\*\* 6.4 mi round trip via Ed's Trail

\- \*\*Elevation gain:\*\* 2,000 ft

\- \*\*Trailhead:\*\* Grouse Vista, Gifford Pinchot NF

\- \*\*Best season:\*\* Summer, for the wildflowers

\- \*\*Permits:\*\* Discover Pass



\## Notes

Incredible view and ruins from an old watch tower
